Ontario Job Grant

The Ontario Job Grant is a Government of Ontario program that can help eligible employers pay for training for current and new employees, delivered by eligible third-party trainers such as registered career colleges.


UWIN Pro does not determine eligibility or approve grants. Please review the official program page and confirm requirements before applying.
